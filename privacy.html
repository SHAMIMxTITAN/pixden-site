Pixden Uploader privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

What the app is

Pixden Uploader is a desktop tool that an independent creator runs on their own computer to publish videos to their own YouTube channels (Pixden and The Replay Cut).

Google data it uses

With the owner's permission, it uses:

  • the YouTube Data API, to upload the owner's videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and visibility, and
  • the YouTube Analytics and YouTube Reporting APIs, read-only, to see the channel's numbers: views, watch time, retention, traffic sources, impressions and click-through rate.

Where it's stored

Sign-in tokens and downloaded reports stay on the owner's computer. The app has no server and sends this data nowhere else.

Sharing

The data isn't sold, shared, transferred or used for advertising. Pixden Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google account permissions. Deleting the app's files on the owner's computer deletes everything it stored.
