Pixden Uploader

Pixden Uploader is a small desktop tool made by an independent video creator to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channels: Pixden (gaming) and The Replay Cut (podcast clips).

What it does

  • Uploads finished videos and Shorts to the channel chosen at Google sign-in.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, category, thumbnail and visibility, and can schedule a publish time.
  • Reads that channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, retention, traffic sources) to plan future videos.

How it uses Google data

The tool signs in with Google and asks for YouTube access only to do the tasks above, on the channel the creator picks. Sign-in tokens stay on the creator's own computer; nothing is sent to any other server, sold or shared. Access can be removed at any time in Google account permissions.

Details are in the privacy policy. This app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
